## Full description (max 4000)
Bao Búa Kéo turns Rock–Paper–Scissors into a one-minute card duel set in a Vietnamese village festival.

HOW TO PLAY
• Each player holds 3 cards: Rock, Paper, Scissors.
• Before the match, each player draws 1 hidden extra card. Your opponent cannot see it.
• Play 4 rounds. Every card is used once. Both players lock a card, then reveal together.
• Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock. A round win is 1 point; most points wins the match.

FEATURES
• Play against bots: each bot has its own habit. Watch, read it and exploit it.
• LAN play with friends: two phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, no Internet needed. Join from the nearby room list, by scanning a QR code or by entering the host address.
• Track the "Played cards" piles to work out what your opponent still holds.
• Drag a card or tap it twice to lock. Comfortable with one hand.
• Folk-art visuals: communal house, festival drum, lion dance, five-colour flags.
• Sound and vibration toggles. Vietnamese and English.

NOTES
• Free to play, contains ads.
• LAN play needs both phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot.
• The camera permission is used only to scan a room QR code; no image is stored or sent.
